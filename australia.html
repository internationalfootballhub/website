/ IFH Australia

A growing region for international football expertise

As one of IFH's three growth regions, Australia represents an opportunity to connect international football expertise — academy development, coach education and player pathways — with a fast-growing local football culture.

Operating from Brisbane, IFH is building relationships with clubs, academies and governing bodies across the country, applying the same activation model used across the network: local presence, one trusted point of contact, and structured delivery.
