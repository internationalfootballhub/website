/ IFH China

A bridge between Chinese schools and international expertise

International Football Hub connects international football expertise with emerging football ecosystems through a trusted and sustainable presence on the ground. In China, IFH supports the cross-border relationship between Chinese football schools and suitable international football and educational partners.

Operating from Shanghai, IFH China acts as the cross-border relationship, coordination and partnership-development platform — identifying the right partners, preparing delegations, and building long-term institutional cooperation.
