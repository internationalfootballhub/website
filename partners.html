/ Partners

Embedded across the football ecosystem

IFH connects three tiers of partners around one activation platform — each gaining a different kind of value from the network.

/ Partnership Tiers

How organisations engage with IFH

01

Anchor Partners

Anchor Partners gain a credible platform to strengthen visibility, positioning and strategic relationships. Their involvement also creates space for exchange, mutual understanding and long-term alignment with relevant football and institutional stakeholders.

02

Core Partners

Core Partners get a practical route to showcase expertise, services or products through real activity on the ground — creating local market engagement that can lead to direct revenue opportunities, partnerships or investors.

03

Expansion Partners

Expansion Partners use the IFH platform to present their product or service live in a practical football environment — helping demonstrate value, build trust and source new clients from the local football ecosystem.
